# Dr. Anastasios Temenos

Hello! My name is **Anastasios Temenos**, though you can call me **Tasos**. I am a Senior Researcher at the **National Technical University of Athens (NTUA)** and **Institute of Communication and Computer Systems (ICCS)**, working on **Remote Sensing, Geoinformatics, and Artificial Intelligence** projects. My research focuses on developing intelligent and trustworthy methods for extracting meaningful information from Earth Observation and geospatial data and translating it into solutions for environmental monitoring, climate resilience, and decision support.

I received my **Diploma in Rural, Surveying and Geoinformatics Engineering** from NTUA in 2021. My diploma thesis focused on automatic building extraction from RGB satellite imagery using deep learning, introducing me to the potential of combining geospatial information with modern computer vision techniques. I then received my **Ph.D. in Geoinformatics and Machine Learning for Earth Observation** from NTUA in 2025. My doctoral research, entitled *“Reshaping Urban Resilience: Trustworthy AI-Driven Frameworks for Strengthening Public Health, Critical Infrastructure and Crisis Management from Climate Change Effects,”* investigated how Earth Observation, geospatial information, and trustworthy artificial intelligence can contribute to understanding and addressing complex climate-related challenges.

## Research Interests

My research interests lie primarily in the integration of **Artificial Intelligence, Earth Observation, and Geospatial Engineering**. In particular, my work focuses on:

- **Machine Learning and Deep Learning for Earth Observation**
- **GeoAI and Geospatial Data Analysis**
- **Explainable and Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence (XAI)**
- **Land Use / Land Cover Mapping**
- **Multispectral and Hyperspectral Image Analysis**
- **Earth Observation Data Cubes**
- **Environmental Monitoring**
- **Climate Change Resilience and Adaptation**
- **Sustainable Urban Planning and Urban Resilience**
- **Computer Vision for Agriculture**

A significant part of my research focuses on making AI models not only accurate, but also **interpretable and trustworthy**, particularly when applied to Earth Observation and environmental data. My work has explored topics including explainable land-cover classification, spectral band analysis and selection, environmental data cubes, flood-event interpretation, urban climate analysis, and AI-assisted environmental monitoring.

Beyond Earth Observation, I am also interested in **computer vision applications for agriculture and precision livestock farming**. My research in this area includes the development and application of vision-based and AI methods for animal monitoring, body condition assessment, and livestock health and welfare.

## Research Projects

Through my work at ICCS and NTUA, I have been actively involved in several collaborative European and international research projects. I have participated in **Horizon 2020, Horizon Europe, and other European Union-funded projects**, as well as the **European Space Agency (ESA)-funded Greek National Satellite Space Project – Axis 3: Land Monitoring Service**.

My responsibilities across these projects include scientific task management and coordination, preparation of technical deliverables and reports, remote sensing and GIS data processing, and the development, training, and validation of AI and computer vision models. I have also participated in **field deployments, system installations, and on-site data collection and validation**, connecting methodological research with real-world implementation.

## Engineering & Teaching

My background as a **Rural, Surveying and Geoinformatics Engineer** provides an important role for my research. In addition to GeoAI and remote sensing, I have professional experience in surveying and geospatial engineering, including field data acquisition using **GNSS receivers and Total Stations**, topographic surveying, Digital Terrain Model generation, and the use of GIS and CAD environments.

Alongside my research activities, I am involved in **academic teaching at NTUA**, supporting undergraduate lectures, tutorials, and laboratory sessions. I also support students in the development of their diploma theses, providing guidance on research design, methodology, implementation, and scientific writing.

## Publications & Recognition

I have authored and co-authored **more than 20 peer-reviewed journal and international conference publications** in the fields of Remote Sensing, GeoAI, explainable AI, environmental monitoring, climate resilience, and computer vision. My research has been presented at international scientific conferences and workshops, including the **IEEE International Geoscience and Remote Sensing Symposium (IGARSS)**.

I also actively contribute to the scientific community as a **peer reviewer for multiple international scientific journals**, including journals published by **IEEE, Elsevier, and MDPI**, primarily within my research fields of Remote Sensing, Earth Observation, Artificial Intelligence, GeoAI, and environmental applications.

My research work has received academic recognition, including the **D. Thomaidis Award** for original scientific publications. My Ph.D. dissertation received the **2026 Dimitris N. Chorafas Foundation Award in the field of Informatics and Computer Science**, recognizing its scientific contribution.
