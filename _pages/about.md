# Dr. Anastasios Temenos

Researcher | ICCS & NTUA

Dr. Anastasios Temenos is a researcher at the Institute of Communication and Computer Systems (ICCS) and the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA), specializing in Earth Observation, remote sensing, GeoAI, explainable artificial intelligence, climate resilience, and environmental monitoring.

He received the Diploma degree in Rural, Surveying and Geoinformatics Engineering from the National Technical University of Athens in 2021 and completed his Ph.D. in Geoinformatics and Machine Learning for Earth Observation, focusing on trustworthy AI-driven frameworks for strengthening public health, critical infrastructure, and crisis management under climate change.

His research focuses on the development of deep learning, machine learning, and explainable AI frameworks for:

- Land Use / Land Cover mapping
- Earth Observation data analytics
- Climate change resilience
- Urban resilience and decision support
- Computer vision applications in agriculture
- Environmental monitoring using geospatial AI

He has authored and co-authored multiple peer-reviewed journal and conference publications and actively participates in European and ESA-funded research projects.
