Hello! I am **Anastasios Temenos**, though you can call me **Tasos**. I am a Senior Researcher affiliated with the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA) and the Institute of Communication and Computer Systems (ICCS), Greece.

I received my integrated M.Eng. in Rural, Surveying and Geoinformatics Engineering from NTUA in 2021 and my PhD in 2025. My doctoral research addressed trustworthy AI approaches to urban resilience, public health, critical infrastructure monitoring and climate-related crisis management.

My work combines Earth observation, machine learning, geospatial analysis and explainable AI. I aim to make advanced methods interpretable, efficient and useful in operational settings, from satellite image understanding and environmental monitoring to computer vision for animal health and welfare.

## Research interests

My interests include GeoAI; optical, radar and UAV remote sensing; explainable and trustworthy AI; semantic segmentation and image understanding; climate resilience and urban sustainability; environmental data cubes and multi-source fusion; precision livestock farming; and efficient AI.

Alongside research, I have contributed to collaborative funded projects through AI model development, data processing, technical deliverables, field installations and validation. I have also supported undergraduate teaching and diploma-thesis work at NTUA.
